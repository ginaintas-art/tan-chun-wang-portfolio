Cleanroom governance & battery quality investigation

Problem
Maintain workshop cleanliness standards and investigate battery quality abnormalities in a controlled manufacturing environment.
My role
Owned the workshop cleanroom build, maintenance coordination and operating rules across a two-year period.
Actions
Supported more than 30 quality-department-issued documents, completed 24 monthly reports, prepared more than three yield-improvement reports and used Zeiss microscopy for environmental particle sampling.
Tools
ISO 9001 · IATF 16949 · Root Cause Analysis · Corrective actions · Zeiss microscopy · Controlled documentation
Outcome
Established sustained cleanroom governance and a documented, evidence-based process for responding to quality abnormalities.
What I learned
How to coordinate three manufacturing sections with safety, technical, maintenance and facilities teams while keeping actions traceable.
RCA Root Cause Analysis identifies why a problem occurred—not only the visible symptom—then connects the cause to corrective action and effectiveness checks.
